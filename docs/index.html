FAQ

Is this official?

No. Fan project, not affiliated with SoundCloud. All music and trademarks belong to SoundCloud and its artists.

Do I need to install anything?

Classic needs WebView2 Runtime, preinstalled on most Win10/11 machines. Alt and Ultra Light need nothing extra on Windows.

Where is my login stored?

In system files, never next to the exe: Classic uses LocalAppData, Alt uses its Electron profile, Ultra Light uses its own LocalAppData folder.

Something broke after a SoundCloud update?

Playback and login are the real site inside the app. If SoundCloud changes its layout, open an issue on GitHub.
